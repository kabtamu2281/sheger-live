## What we collect

- **Account data:** phone number or email address, password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider), Google account ID if you sign in with Google, date of birth, username, display name, bio, profile and cover photos, interests and language.
- **Activity:** follows, blocks, live streams you start or join, chat messages, gifts, fan clubs, events, reports and appeals, and the time you spend in rooms (used to count viewers).
- **Direct messages:** the messages you send and receive, so they can be delivered and so reported messages can be reviewed.
- **Wallet and payments:** coin purchases, gift and ledger history, payout requests, payout account details (phone or bank account number and account holder name) and identity verification status. Card and wallet details entered at Google Play or Chapa are handled by those providers, not by us.
- **Device data:** a push notification token if push is enabled, app version, and technical logs needed to keep the service secure.
- **Location:** we do not collect GPS or precise location. If you turn on Nearby, we store only the city or region you choose.
- **Camera and microphone:** used only while you preview, go live or sit in a guest seat. Live audio and video pass through our streaming provider in real time. We do not record streams.

## How we use it

- To run your account, live rooms, chat, gifts, wallet and payouts.
- To keep people safe: enforcing age limits, reviewing reports, preventing fraud and spam, and complying with the law.
- To recommend lives. You can turn off personalized recommendations in Privacy settings.
- To send notifications you have allowed.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** (database, authentication, file storage and server functions).
- **Agora** (real-time audio and video delivery).
- **Google** (Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In, and Firebase Cloud Messaging when push is enabled).
- **Chapa** (payments, only in builds where it is enabled).
- **Payout partners** such as telebirr, CBE Birr, M-Pesa or banks, to pay creators.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm.

Other users can see your public profile, your public lives and your chat messages in rooms you join. If your account is private, only approved followers see your followers and past lives.

We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

- Profile and activity data are kept while your account exists.
- When you delete your account, we remove your profile details, follows, direct messages you sent, fan club memberships, push tokens and viewing history, and replace your room chat messages with "[removed]".
- Financial records (purchases, gifts, ledger entries and payouts) are kept in pseudonymised form for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- Moderation records and audit logs are kept to handle appeals, repeat offenders and legal requests.

## Your rights

You can view and edit your profile, download a copy of your data (Settings, Download my data), limit who can message you, block people, make your account private, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights.

## Children

Ethio Live is not for anyone under 16. Users aged 16 and 17 cannot go live, buy coins, send paid gifts or request payouts, and can only receive direct messages from people they follow. If you believe a child under 16 is using Ethio Live, report the account or contact us.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to personal data is limited by role, sensitive staff actions require two-step verification, and staff actions are recorded in an audit log.

## Changes

We will tell you in the app about material changes to this policy.

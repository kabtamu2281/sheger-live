# Ethio Live Community Guidelines

Ethio Live is a place to share Ethiopian culture, music, conversation and talent. These rules apply to live streams, audio rooms, chat, direct messages, profiles, fan clubs and gifts.

## Zero tolerance

These lead to immediate removal and a permanent ban, and may be reported to authorities:

- Any sexual content involving minors, or sexualising minors in any way.
- Grooming, or asking a minor for images, contact details or to meet.
- Sharing intimate images of anyone without their consent.
- Human trafficking, sexual services or exploitation.
- Credible threats of violence, terrorism or promotion of violent groups.

## Not allowed

- **Nudity and sexual content**, including sexual acts, exposed genitals, and sexually explicit performances.
- **Hate speech** attacking people for ethnicity, nation, religion, gender, disability or other protected traits. Ethnic slurs and calls for violence between communities are removed.
- **Harassment and bullying**, including targeted insults, doxxing, stalking and encouraging others to pile on.
- **Violence and dangerous acts**, including graphic violence, self-harm content and dangerous challenges.
- **Scams and fraud**, including fake giveaways, asking for coins in exchange for off-platform promises, selling accounts, gift manipulation and fake payout offers.
- **Impersonation** of other people, creators, brands or Ethio Live staff.
- **Illegal activity**, including drugs, weapons sales and gambling.
- **Spam**, including repetitive messages, unsolicited promotion and fake engagement.

## Minors

You must be 16 or older to use Ethio Live and 18 or older to go live. Minors may not appear on stream as the main subject. If you see a child at risk, use Report and choose "A minor may be at risk"; these reports are reviewed first.

## Gifts and money

- Never ask viewers to send gifts in exchange for sexual content or private contact.
- Do not promise money, jobs or prizes you cannot deliver.
- Staff will never ask for your password or a verification code.

## Hosts and moderators

Hosts are responsible for their rooms. Use mute, remove and block tools, and appoint moderators you trust. A host who repeatedly allows violations in their room may lose broadcasting access.

## Enforcement

Depending on how serious a violation is and whether it has happened before, we may remove content, end a live stream, mute you in chat, restrict broadcasting, suspend your account or ban it. Repeat offenders face stronger action. Rooms ended for a violation show viewers that the live was removed.

## Reporting and appeals

Report any live, message or profile from its menu. Reports are confidential. If we take action on your account, you will see it in the Safety center and can appeal once. A different moderator reviews every appeal.

# Google Play store listing

## English

**App name:** Ethio Live

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Go live, chat and send gifts with creators across Ethiopia and the diaspora.

**Full description:**

Ethio Live is a live video and audio community made for Ethiopia.

Watch live: music, comedy, culture, coffee-ceremony chats and more from creators in Addis Ababa, Hawassa, Bahir Dar, Mekelle, Dire Dawa and around the diaspora.

Go live yourself: start a video live or an audio room in seconds, invite guests on stage, and grow your fans.

PK battles: challenge another host to a timed battle. Gifts from fans decide the winner, scored fairly by our servers.

Gifts with a local touch: send a jebena, a buna cup, an adey flower or a golden lion to show support.

Earn as a creator: creators 18 and over can turn the gifts they receive into earnings paid to telebirr, CBE Birr, M-Pesa or a bank account, after identity verification.

Fan clubs, rankings and events: join your favourite host's fan club, climb the weekly rankings and take part in seasonal events.

Safe by design: report and block tools on every live and message, private accounts, message controls, and extra protections for users under 18.

Available in English and Amharic, with Afaan Oromo coming.

Coins are virtual items used only to send gifts. Ethio Live is for users aged 16 and over; live streaming, purchases and payouts are for users aged 18 and over.
